Support

Questions, answered.

How long does shipping take?

Free worldwide shipping, typically 6–12 business days. You’ll get a tracking link by email the moment your parcel is scanned by the carrier.

Where do you ship from?

We ship from our fulfilment partners in Asia and Europe. That’s how we keep shipping free and prices where they are.

What if it doesn’t work for me?

Email us within 30 days and we’ll refund you in full — you don’t need a reason, and you don’t pay return postage.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. All payments are processed by Stripe, which is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified. Your card number is never sent to us or stored on our site.

Do you take returns on opened items?

Yes. Opened, used, washed — it doesn’t matter. If it didn’t help, we’d rather have it back than have you keep something useless.
