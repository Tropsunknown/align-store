Returns

Last updated 4 November 2026

Thirty days, no questions. If a product doesn't earn its place in your routine, we'd rather refund you than have you keep it.

How to return something

  • Email us within 30 days of delivery with your order reference and what went wrong.
  • We reply within one business day with a return address and instructions.
  • Send it back — used or opened is fine, we only ask that it isn't damaged deliberately.
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5 business days of the parcel arriving back with us.

Return postage

If the item is faulty, or we shipped the wrong thing, we cover postage. For a change of mind, return postage is yours — unless the order was over $50, in which case we'll refund it with the item.

Faulty or damaged

Send a photo. We replace or refund immediately — no need to post the item back for anything under $50.

Refund timing

Stripe processes refunds in 5–10 business days depending on your bank. You'll get an email from Stripe the moment it's issued.
